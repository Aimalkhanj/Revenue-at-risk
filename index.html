Data

UCI Machine Learning Repository, “Online Retail II” (Chen, 2019). 1,067,371 transaction lines from a UK-based online gift retailer, Dec 2009 – Dec 2011. Many customers are wholesalers.

Cleaning

    Method

    • Fiscal year runs Dec–Nov so both years are complete.
    • RFM: quintile scores on recency, frequency and spend; snapshot 10 Dec 2011.
    • Overdue: silence > 2× the customer’s own median gap (min 60 days), under 365 days.
    • Repeat vs first order: chi-square test of independence.
